## 📱 Features & 15 Calculators Highlighted

The website showcases the complete suite of **15 financial calculators** divided into 3 intuitive categories:

### 1. Investment & Wealth (7 Calculators)
1. **Simple Interest** — Fast calculation for loans and deposits with tenure in years, months, or days.
2. **Compound Interest** — Multi-frequency compounding (Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annually) with visual wealth breakdown.
3. **Chit Fund Calculator** — Monthly auction dividend, commission deductions, and net payable amount.
4. **Fixed Deposit (FD)** — Cumulative and payout options with maturity value and total interest earned.
5. **Recurring Deposit (RD)** — Monthly installment compounding with maturity value projections.
6. **SIP Calculator** — Systematic Investment Plan returns, total wealth accumulation, and invested vs earned comparison.
7. **ROI & Rule of 72** — Return on investment percentage, annualized returns, and doubling time estimation.

### 2. Loan & Debt (4 Calculators)
8. **Loan EMI Calculator** — Monthly installment calculation, total interest payable, and full payment breakdown.
9. **Prepayment Simulator** — Simulate multiple lump-sum and recurring prepayments to calculate tenure reduction and interest saved.
10. **Loan Comparison** — Side-by-side evaluation of two loan options with clear lowest-cost winner indicators.
11. **Flat vs Reducing Rate** — Uncovers the true effective interest rate behind flat-rate dealer and loan schemes.

### 3. General & Everyday (4 Calculators)
12. **GST Calculator** — Dual-mode tax calculation (GST Inclusive and GST Exclusive) with CGST / SGST split.
13. **Discount Calculator** — Original price, percentage discount, total savings, and final payable bill.
14. **Percentage Calculator** — Instant percentage of value, percentage increase/decrease, and ratios.
15. **Tip & Split Calculator** — Tip computation and multi-person bill splitting for dining and shared expenses.

## 🌟 App Highlights & Superpowers

- **Official PDF Statements & Reports**: One-tap generation and sharing of branded financial summaries and amortization schedules via `expo-print` & `expo-sharing`.
- **Excel / CSV Export**: Export comprehensive year-by-year and month-by-month payment schedules for spreadsheet analysis.
- **Interactive Visual Amortization Schedules**: Dynamic dual-color progress bars tracking loan repayment milestones in real-time.
- **Privacy-First & 100% Offline**: All calculations run locally on the device with zero cloud tracking, no sign-in requirements, and complete data privacy.

## 🚀 How to Preview & Deploy

### Local Preview
Simply double-click `index.html` or open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No build step, node modules, or local server required.

### Free Static Hosting Options
You can deploy this static site in seconds to:
- **GitHub Pages**: Push this directory to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages under **Settings > Pages**.
- **Cloudflare Pages / Vercel / Netlify**: Connect your repository or drag-and-drop the `finance-calculator-website` folder directly onto the dashboard.
- **Firebase Hosting**: Run `firebase init hosting` and set the public folder to this directory.
