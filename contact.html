Reporting an Issue or Bug

Encountered a problem or incorrect calculation? Please include the following details in your email to help us diagnose and fix it quickly:

  • 1
    Device Information Your device model (e.g., Pixel 7, Galaxy S23) and Android OS version.
  • 2
    Calculator Used Specify whether the issue occurred in Simple Interest, Compound Interest, EMI, GST, Chit, Prepayment, etc.
  • 3
    Inputs & Expected Result Share the specific numbers entered, what result you observed, and what was expected.
  • 4
    Screenshots (Optional) Attaching a screenshot helps us resolve issues much faster.
