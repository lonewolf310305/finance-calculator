Financial Disclaimer

Important Notice Regarding Financial Calculations, Bank Rates & Estimations

1. General Informational Purpose Only

Crucial Notice: All calculations, results, schedules, tables, interest quotes, dividend figures, and projections generated by Finance Calculator (both within the mobile application and on this website) are provided exclusively for informational, illustrative, and educational purposes. They do not constitute official financial advice, certified loan sanction documents, banking promises, or accounting statements.

2. Estimates vs. Actual Financial Institution Results

While every mathematical formula implemented inside Finance Calculator is carefully verified against standard mathematical models and standard industry practices, actual figures provided by commercial banks, housing finance corporations (HFCs), NBFCs, micro-lenders, chit fund companies, or mutual fund asset managers will often differ.

Discrepancies can occur due to several real-world banking factors, including:

  • Day-Count Conventions: Banks and lenders frequently employ different calendar methods (e.g., Actual/365, Actual/360, or 30/360) for daily interest accrual, which produce minor variations in monthly interest charges.
  • Compounding Periods & Cut-Off Times: Fixed Deposit (FD) and Recurring Deposit (RD) compounding frequencies (quarterly vs. annual compounding) and bank-specific cut-off dates can influence final maturity sums.
  • Ancillary Fees & Charges: Official loan repayment schedules from banks incorporate loan processing fees, GST on documentation charges, stamp duties, credit insurance premiums, documentation charges, and loan pre-closure fees that are not part of pure mathematical interest equations.
  • Floating & Benchmark Linked Rates: Real-world bank home loans and vehicle loans typically follow floating Repo-Linked Lending Rates (RLLR) or External Benchmark Lending Rates (EBLR) that fluctuate during your repayment tenure. Finance Calculator provides fixed-tenure projections based on your entered parameters.
  • Chit Fund Foremen Margins & Rules: In auction chit schemes, actual dividend distribution, foreman commission deductions, GST on foreman commission, and net payout cash depend on registered state chit regulations and specific chit company bye-laws.

3. No Professional Financial or Investment Advice

Nothing contained in Finance Calculator or on this website constitutes a financial recommendation, investment advice, tax counseling, or loan solicitation.

Always consult a qualified, registered Financial Advisor (SEBI registered in India, or your local jurisdiction's equivalent regulatory body), a Chartered Accountant (CA), or your lender's official loan sanction letter before taking financial decisions.
