Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 • App: Finance Calculator • Developer: RR Production

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Finance Calculator mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), developed and published by RR Production ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Finance Calculator is designed as a standalone on-device utility to perform practical, everyday financial calculations directly on your mobile device without collecting or storing personal financial credentials.

2. Information We Collect

Key Summary: Finance Calculator does not require you to create an account, register personal credentials, or link any financial accounts or bank accounts.

Personal Information: We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, government ID, or banking login credentials.

Calculation Inputs: The numbers, amounts, interest rates, and durations you input into the calculators (such as Simple Interest, Compound Interest, EMI, GST, Chit, or Prepayment) are processed locally on your device in real-time. This calculation data is never transmitted to external cloud servers.

Technical & Diagnostic Data: Standard non-identifying diagnostic data (such as anonymized crash logs provided via Google Play Console) may be generated to help maintain application stability.

3. How Information Is Used

Any data processed by the App is used strictly for:

  • Executing mathematical calculations and presenting accurate financial figures on your screen.
  • Maintaining your temporary calculation history locally on your device (where enabled).
  • Troubleshooting application stability, crash fixes, and technical maintenance.
  • Ensuring compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies.

4. Data Storage

Finance Calculator functions on-device. Your calculation entries and history are stored locally in your device's isolated application storage sandbox.

We do not maintain remote cloud databases of your financial calculations. If you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App from your device, any locally stored calculation history is deleted by your operating system.

5. Third-Party Services

The App interfaces only with standard platform services required for mobile distribution.

The App may display advertisements provided by trusted ad networks (such as Google AdMob) to support development. These third-party networks may process non-personally identifiable device identifiers in strict compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

6. Google Play Services

Finance Calculator is distributed via the Google Play Store. The App may utilize Google Play Services for distribution, app updates, in-app reviews, or standard platform support. Google's collection and processing of data through Google Play Services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Finance Calculator is a general utility intended for general audiences. It does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Data Security

Because Finance Calculator processes your calculations locally on your Android device, your data is protected by the built-in security architecture of your Android operating system (such as application sandboxing and device-level encryption).

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

RR Production may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or application enhancements.

Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App following modifications indicates acceptance of those updates.
