The Simple Interest calculator uses the standard Indian lending (vaddi) method: Interest = Principal × Rate × Time. It applies the 30-day month / 360-day year convention used in informal lending. You can input rates as % per month or % per year, enter exact from-to calendar dates, and the daily accrual rate is also displayed.

Many car dealers and retail lenders quote a "flat rate" of 6–8% which sounds attractive — but is calculated on the original principal for the entire tenure, making it far costlier. Our tool converts the quoted flat rate into the equivalent reducing balance rate, revealing the true cost. A "6% flat" loan often equals ~11–12% reducing — a massive hidden markup.
